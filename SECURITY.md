# Security policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to **support@octron.ai**. Do not open a public issue. Include:

- the affected repository, version, or commit;
- concise reproduction steps;
- the potential impact;
- logs or screenshots with secrets and personal data removed.

Do not send credentials, session transcripts, customer data, private artifacts or signing material.

If the affected repository has its own security policy, follow that policy instead.

## Disclosure

We will acknowledge complete reports and coordinate investigation, remediation and disclosure. Please allow a reasonable remediation window before publishing details; we do not request indefinite nondisclosure.
